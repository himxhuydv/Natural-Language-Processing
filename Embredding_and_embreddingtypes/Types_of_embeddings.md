# Types of Embeddings
1.**Static**: each word has the fixed vector representation no matters where it appears.**One word=one meaning(fixed)**
running(run),better(good)
2.**Contextual**:word meaning changes based on sentense context
for example 
bank("river bank")
bank("money bank)
3.**Sentence/document Embedding**:instead of words we convert the whole sentence or paragraoh into one vector 
for ex:
"i love coding"-one vector
