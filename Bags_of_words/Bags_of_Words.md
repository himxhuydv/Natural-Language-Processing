# Bag of words
The bag of words have a unique list of vocabulary and  the frequency of occuring of words .At the time when we are making the list we include the 
* noun 
* verbs
* adjective
* etc
**remove the duplicacy**
for ex:
Text
"I love AI and i love coding"
in this we make the unique vocabulary list 
words  Frequency
I-      2
love-   2
AI-     1
and-    1
coding- 1
*the frequency is basically the vector representation of words.*
now the vector then  become the feature vector and goes to the model as input for the model learning.
