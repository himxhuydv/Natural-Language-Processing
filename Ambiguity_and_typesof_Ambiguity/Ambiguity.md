# Ambiguity
Ambiguity is basically a word or a sentence that has more than one meaning.
**Ambiguity in Natural Language Processing**:
In the NLP  the Ambiguity happens because human langauge is such a flexible language.So,that's why it literally can have multiple meaning due to the intent,accent,situation,context,structure and usage.
for ex:
1.**I have a bat**
here as you can see that he have bat but that doesn't explain which kind of bat are we talking about is the cricket bat or animal bat.
there are two interpretation 
1.the cricket bat
2.Animal bat
