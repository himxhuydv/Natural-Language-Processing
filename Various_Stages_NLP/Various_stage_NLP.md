# Various Stages of NLP
NLP is divided into multiple stages.So,when the sentenses comes it basically goes  through these certain stages and then machine fully understand the true meaning of sentense.
1.**Lexical Analysis**:The process of Split the whole sentense into token or single words,basic unit.
2.**Syntatic Analysis**:The process of analysing the grammatical structure structure of a sentense.
3.**Semantifc Analysis**:The process of understanding the proper meaning of the sentense.
4.**Pragmentic Analysis**:The proccess of understanding context analysis 
5.**Discourse Analysis**:The process of checking and connecting it with differenet sentense if it connected.
