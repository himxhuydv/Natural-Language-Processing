# Phrase structure or Constituency Grammar
the way of describing a sentence by breaking into a smaller meaningfull parts(phrases).
we show that how in the linguistic how the sentences are formed using the phrases(parts).then we group the phrase to meaningfull phrase.**This all done just for showing how words are formed**
# Constituents
basically group of word that function as a unit.
Phrase Type
1.NP(Noun Phrase)
2.VP(Verb Phrase)
3.PP(prepositional Phrase)
4.AdjP(Adjective Phrase)
5.AdvP(Adverb Phrase)
**Rules**
 * S->NP Vp( a sentence=noun phrase+verb phrase)
 * Np->Det N(np can consist of determiner+noun)
 * Vp-> V NP(vp can consist of verb + noun Phrase)

## Phrase Structure or Constituency Grammer
for ex:
"The boy is playing football"
Step 1: Group the words
1.Noun Phrase(NP)
"The boy" # here we are talking about the person.
2.Verb Phrase(VP)
"is playing football"# here we are talking about what the person is doing.
**Sentence=Np+VP**
[The boy] + [is playing football] # here as you can see we have created that into the meaningfull phrases.
